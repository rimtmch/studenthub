# 🏥 RMCH Student Hub

**The ultimate digital companion for RMCH medical students.**

Welcome to the **RMCH Student Hub**, a comprehensive progressive web application (PWA) designed to streamline your academic life and campus experience. Built with a stunning "Liquid Glass" aesthetic, this app brings everything a medical student needs into one fluid, beautiful interface.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Smart Attendance Manager
Never worry about falling below 75% again.
*   **Theory vs. Practical:** Track attendance separately for every subject.
*   **Smart Calculator:** Instantly see how many classes you can safely "bunk" or how many you need to attend to reach the safe zone.
*   **Visual Indicators:** Color-coded stats (Green for Safe, Red for Danger) keep you informed at a glance.

### 🤝 Peer-to-Peer Book Renting
A convenient campus sharing platform.
*   **Easy Listing:** List your medical books for rent or borrow from fellow students instantly.
*   **Request Board:** Manage borrow requests, approve handovers, and track book status.
*   **Safe Platform:** Easy internal communication and verification.

### ❄️ The Chill Zone
The campus social pulse.
*   **Anonymous Confessions:** Share your thoughts freely without revealing your identity.
*   **Identity Toggle:** Choose to post as "Anonymous" or show your username and avatar.
*   **Interactive Community:** Like posts, reply to threads, and engage with your peers.
*   **Safety First:** Built-in reporting system and moderation tools.

### 🍽️ Daily Mess Menu
Know what's cooking before you leave your room.
*   **Live Updates:** View Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, and Dinner menus for the current day.
*   **Admin Managed:** The student committee keeps the menu up-to-date.

### 📚 Digital Library
A pocket-sized repository of knowledge.
*   **Quick Search:** Find books by title, author, or subject instantly.
*   **Subject Filters:** Narrow down resources by year or specialty.

### 🎨 Stunning UI/UX
*   **Liquid Glass Theme:** A modern, frosted-glass interface that looks beautiful on any device.
*   **Dark & Light Modes:** Switch between "Deep Ocean" (Dark) and "Clear Water" (Light) themes.
*   **PWA Support:** Install it as a native app on iOS, Android, Mac, or PC.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security
*   **Secure Login:** PIN-based authentication.
*   **Profile Management:** Update your avatar, email, and details securely.
*   **Username Lock:** Prevents frequent username changes to maintain community trust.
